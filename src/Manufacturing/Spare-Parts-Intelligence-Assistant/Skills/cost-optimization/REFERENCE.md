# Reference: Cost Optimization

## Reusability Notes

The cost-of-action vs. cost-of-inaction framing, budget-fit checking against a cost center, and order-consolidation detection are general procurement-optimization patterns directly reusable by Procurement (indirect spend optimization) and Production Planning (raw material buy-ahead decisions). The requisition-drafting and approval-tier-routing logic is reusable by any department with a tiered SAP MM/ERP approval hierarchy, simply by re-mapping criticality class definitions.
