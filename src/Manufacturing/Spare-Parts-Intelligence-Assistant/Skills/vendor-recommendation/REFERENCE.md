# Reference: Vendor Recommendation

## Reusability Notes

The multi-criteria vendor-ranking logic (price/lead-time/OTIF weighted by criticality) and the embedding-based alternative-part matcher are directly reusable by Procurement for general indirect/direct sourcing decisions, and by any department managing multi-vendor MRO categories (Facilities, Utilities). The compatibility-validation gate — never auto-approving a substitute that a vendor's own notes flag as requiring engineering review — is a pattern worth carrying into any AI-assisted substitution workflow regardless of part category.
